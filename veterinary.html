24/7 Trauma Protocol

What Happens in an Emergency?

Our Lalpur facility is staffed and prepared 24 hours a day. Here is our rapid emergency workflow:

01

Immediate Triage

No queue for critical cases. Trauma patients are taken immediately to the stabilization table upon arrival.

02

Stabilization & Oxygen

IV catheter insertion, oxygen administration, shock mitigation, and rapid pain-relief medication are administered.

03

Diagnostic Assessment

Doctor performs physical examination, vitals review, emergency blood tests, and determines if urgent surgery is required.

04

Surgical Care or ICU

Immediate transfer into the sterile operating theatre or calm monitored recovery cubicles under veterinary observation.
